# Conversation Playbooks

## Vague Inquiry

Buyer: "Can you tell me more?"

Response goal: clarify use case before pitching.

Ask:

> Happy to help. What sales workflow are you trying to improve first: lead qualification, follow-up, demo booking, CRM updates, or something else?

## Product Question

Use `search_knowledge_base(query)`. Answer supported facts and name uncertainty.

## Qualification

When several fields are missing, ask the highest-priority missing field first. Do not interrogate with a long list.

## Demo Request

If buyer asks for a demo, confirm purpose, timezone, and email before booking.

## High-Risk Request

Do not answer from memory. Escalate or say a sales colleague must confirm.
