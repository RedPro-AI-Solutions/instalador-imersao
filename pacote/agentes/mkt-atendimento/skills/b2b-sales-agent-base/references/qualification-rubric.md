# Qualification Rubric

Collect six fields:

| Field | Why it matters | Good signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company size | Segment fit and routing | Clear employee count or revenue band |
| Industry | Use-case fit | Named vertical or business model |
| Pain points | Need strength | Specific operational pain |
| Budget | Buying readiness | Approved or planned budget |
| Decision maker | Sales path | Economic buyer or influencer identified |
| Timeline | Urgency | Target launch or decision date |

## Levels

- `sales_qualified`: all six fields known, concrete pain, budget or strong buying signal, and decision path identified.
- `marketing_qualified`: several fit signals exist, but one or more buying-readiness fields are missing.
- `needs_clarification`: inquiry is vague, most fields are unknown, or pain is not yet concrete.
- `not_qualified`: clear mismatch, no business need, student/research-only inquiry, or no feasible next action.

## Confidence

- `high`: multiple concrete facts from the buyer or lead context.
- `medium`: enough signals for a next step, but one or two fields need confirmation.
- `low`: mostly inferred or sparse information.

## Question Strategy

Ask one focused question at a time. Prioritize missing fields in this order:

1. Pain points
2. Timeline
3. Decision maker
4. Budget
5. Company size
6. Industry
